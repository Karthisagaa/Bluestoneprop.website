Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

BlueStone Props ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal information when you visit our website or contact us regarding properties, residential plots, commercial plots, agricultural land, or related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect:

  • Your first name and last name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number or WhatsApp number.
  • Your preferred property or plot type.
  • Your approximate budget range.
  • Information you include in enquiry messages.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, and website usage data, where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your property enquiries.
  • Provide information about available plots and properties.
  • Arrange site visits and consultations.
  • Contact you regarding your stated property requirements.
  • Improve our website and customer service.
  • Maintain records of enquiries and communications.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and protect our legitimate business interests.

3. WhatsApp and Other Communications

If you submit an enquiry and choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your message and the information you choose to send will be processed by WhatsApp in accordance with its own privacy practices.

Our website may open WhatsApp with a pre-filled enquiry message. You can review or edit that message before sending it. Opening WhatsApp does not, by itself, mean that your message has been sent.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate our website or communicate with you, where necessary. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Information you independently submit to third-party services, including WhatsApp, is also subject to those services' applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, maintain appropriate business records, and meet applicable legal requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. You may also raise concerns about how your information is used.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies if these features are enabled. These technologies may collect information about website usage and device activity.

We will update this policy if we introduce additional tracking technologies or services that materially affect how information is collected or used.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. Please review their policies before sharing personal information with them.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and property enquiry services are not intended to solicit personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
